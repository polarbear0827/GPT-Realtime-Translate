# Keynote Live Translator v0.5.0

Translate what your Mac is playing, shown as floating subtitles over any app, plus a light mode.

## Highlights

- **Translate videos and meetings playing on your Mac** — choose 音源 > 電腦播放的聲音. The app captures
  the audio digitally (no speakers or microphone needed, headphones are fine) and can mute the original
  while you read the translation. Requires macOS 14.2 or later.
- **Floating captions (懸浮字幕)** — a frosted-glass subtitle bar that stays on top of everything,
  including full-screen YouTube: large translated text with the original underneath. Drag it anywhere,
  resize it, change the text size, or lock it so clicks pass through to the video.
- **Light mode** — 設定 > 顯示 > 外觀: follow system, light, or dark.
- **Green screen that you can see** — the black / green caption background now shows immediately.

## First use of system audio

macOS asks for permission to record system audio the first time. If captions stay empty, open
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording, allow Keynote Live Translator
under "System Audio Recording Only", and reopen the app.

Tip: for videos in another language, "只顯示翻譯" or 雙語 with "翻譯模型附帶的原文" detects the spoken
language automatically, so you don't need to set the original language.

## Install

Download `KeynoteLiveTranslator-0.5.0.pkg` (macOS 12.3 or later; system audio needs 14.2+).
